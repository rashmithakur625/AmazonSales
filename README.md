# AmazonSales

Data analytics project showcasing using Power BI


## Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Amazon's sales performance across categories, products, and time periods. The dashboard consolidates raw transactional data into a single, easy-to-navigate view, allowing stakeholders to track revenue trends, monitor product performance, and identify top-performing categories at a glance. It was designed with a dark, high-contrast theme to make key metrics and visual trends stand out clearly for quick, confident decision-making.

## Key Metrics
At the top of the dashboard, four summary cards give an instant snapshot of overall business performance: Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales of $2.18M, Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Sales of $811.09K, YTD Products Sold totaling 27.75K units, and YTD Reviews reaching 19.42M. These KPIs provide a quick pulse check on sales volume, revenue, and customer engagement before diving into deeper analysis.

## Sales Trends
The dashboard includes two time-based visualizations to help track performance over different periods. The "Sales by Month" area chart highlights seasonal patterns, showing a steady climb through the year with a sharp spike around September and again toward November-December, reflecting peak shopping seasons. The "Sales by Week" bar chart offers a more granular view, revealing consistent weekly growth with a strong surge in the final quarter of the year, useful for identifying short-term demand spikes and planning inventory or marketing pushes accordingly.

## Category & Product Performance
A detailed breakdown table, "Sales by Product Category," compares YTD Sales, QTD Sales, and percentage growth across categories such as Camera, Car Accessories, Men's Clothes, Men's Shoes, Mobile & Accessories, and Toys — with Men's Shoes emerging as the top-performing category at $940,266 in YTD sales and a 43.18% growth rate. Complementing this, the "Top 5 Products YTD Sales" chart ranks individual bestsellers like Nikon and Atomos products, while the "Top 5 Products YTD Reviews" chart highlights the most-reviewed items, led by SanDisk products with reviews in the hundreds of thousands, offering insight into both sales performance and customer engagement at the product level.

## Interactivity
To make the dashboard flexible for different analysis needs, it includes dynamic filters for Product Category and Quarter, allowing users to drill down into specific segments and time frames without needing separate reports. A dedicated "Details" panel button is also included for accessing extended, granular data on demand.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** — Data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualization
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation** — Power Query for shaping raw sales data
- **Dashboard Design** — Custom dark theme with card-based KPIs, trend charts, and ranked bar visuals

## Purpose
This dashboard was built to simulate a real-world business intelligence use case — turning raw e-commerce sales data into a clear, actionable reporting tool that supports faster, data-driven decisions around inventory, marketing, and category-level strategy.
